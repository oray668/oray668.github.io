Rocket Light 2025
A light made from a galvanized sheet metal duct found in a local skip, reimagined using bits (angle brackets, a U channel, nuts and bolts) picked up during wanders through Bunnings aisles. Exhibited in the Bypass Call for Light exhibition with 20 other lights.

Space for Free 2024
Space for Free is a poster-zine documenting spaces around the world that are accessible to anyone, for any use, for free. We investigated, drew and wrote about the Okahu Bay Wharf, a micro-community of fisherpeople, families, exercisers, tourists and other curious visitors to Tāmaki Makaurau Auckland's Tamaki Drive.

Produced by Stephanie Davidson (Davidson Rafailidis), Eira Roberts and Roxana Cordon-Ibanez, with printing by Vide Press and graphic design by Stahl R and Kathrin Baumgartner. Supported by the Graham Foundation.

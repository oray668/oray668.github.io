Rendered Futures 2025
Drawings from my MArch(Prof) thesis featured in the 2025 exhibition Rendered Futures at Objectspace, first in Auckland and then in Christchurch. I also contributed to a panel discussion considering the evolution of architectural drawing as it becomes increasingly codified through computer-aided design.

Curated by Kim Paton and Micheal McCabe.

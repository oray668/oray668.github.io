Call for Light 2025
In 2024, Bypass sent out a call for light, an invitation to friends and those in our community of recent architectural graduates to come together with the shared purpose of making light. During a series of gatherings over the next year, we met to discuss, catch up and develop our lights together.

Our final exhibition, held in November 2025, celebrated that year of collective dreaming and making, curated within Ron Sang’s Choice Plaza tower, itself a sort of lantern in the middle of Auckland city. Memories from the building’s past: the hopes of its developer, the tumultuous highs and lows of its first few decades and our fond recollections of dining here during university were illuminated by a constellation of new additions.

With lights by Calvin Feng, Plum Li, Binh Minh Ha, Leith Macfarlane, Izzy Ray-Chaudhuri, Pat Diswat, Vinayak Garg, Michelle Wang, Nicola Chang, Daniel Ho, Philip Lee, Yeri Lee, Eugene van Dalen, Jack Wu, Billy Wallis and me.
